FAQ

Common questions

Is my financial data safe?
Absolutely. SubSentry processes everything locally on your device. We never see, store, or transmit your payment information. Your data never leaves your browser.
How does auto-detection work?
SubSentry recognizes subscription confirmation pages and payment receipts from popular services like Netflix, Spotify, Adobe, and 50+ platforms. When you visit these pages, it automatically logs the subscription details.
Can I add subscriptions manually?
Yes! While auto-detection catches most subscriptions, you can always add, edit, or remove subscriptions manually from the dashboard.
Does it work with annual subscriptions?
Yes. SubSentry tracks both monthly and annual subscriptions, and will remind you before annual renewals so you can decide whether to continue.
What's included in the free plan?
The free plan includes tracking up to 3 subscriptions, auto-detection, basic dashboard, and currency conversion. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited tracking and advanced features.
